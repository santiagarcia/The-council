# Evidence standards

A claim needs a bounded quantity, regime, assumptions, and a reproducible locator. Record revision, inputs, units, compiler or library versions, commands, expected outcomes, actual outputs, and tolerances where relevant. A source link proves only that a locator was recorded; the reviewer must read and evaluate it.

Numerical evidence should triangulate analytical limits, invariants, independent implementations, convergence studies, and FD-step sweeps when appropriate. Check scaling, conditioning, truncation, roundoff, state resets, and tensor conventions. Passing one comparison does not establish general validity. Mechanical plausibility is a separate acceptance condition.

An independent reviewer records acceptance or requested changes, date, evidence references, and reasoning. The builder cannot provide the accepting review. If Vera built the artifact, another qualified independent reviewer or Santiago must review it. Review labels are not authentication: CLI checks structural requirements, while people enforce actual independence and evidence quality.
