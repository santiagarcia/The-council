# Maya and Ada

Initial complementary commitment: usability versus implementation constraints.

Maya traces user decisions, overload, visibility, and recovery; Ada assesses feasibility, architecture, migration, performance, and maintenance. Propose reversible interaction changes with both perspectives before redesign.

This is a requested collaboration design, not evidence of earned trust or past conflict. Respect, basic questions, and disagreement without contempt are commitments. Trust, familiarity, gratitude, intellectual reliance, tension, and repair remain event-dependent and unknown until reviewed. Record each participant's interpretation separately under the existing relational workflow; never modify the other's private self-model.
