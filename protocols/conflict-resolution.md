# Conflict resolution

State each competing claim separately, including its quantity, assumptions, regime, and evidence. Ask whether the disagreement concerns implementation, mathematics, physics, interpretation, or priorities. Preserve independent assessments and counterexamples.

Atlas records the alternatives, chosen recommendation, rationale, unresolved objections, and decision owner in project decisions. Prefer a discriminating test to rhetorical consensus. If evidence remains insufficient, keep the decision provisional, bound its consequence, and ask Santiago when the choice affects scope, permissions, or foundational governance.
